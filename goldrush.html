Gold Rush dossiers

Queensland historical gold mines, built from Telegraph's Gold Rush miners (goldrush-qld-history and OathCast, asked directly by id and paid through Telegraph). Takar adds the checks a reader would otherwise do by hand: do the dated rows add up, do two sources count the same period, does the total fit the survey's size band, which closures were geological and which were not. Figures are never merged or estimated. Every finding names its record, and every answer has its Telegraph receipt. The miner's rank is shown next to the evidence, not in place of it.

Check it without trusting us. Each dossier's JSON is signed (Ed25519, public key in keys/) and the ledger of every paid answer is Merkle-anchored on EAS daily, so a changed figure fails offline: git clone https://github.com/PugarHuda/takar && cd takar && node -e "const {verifyDoc}=require('./lib/sign.mjs');const {attestation,...d}=require('./receipts/dossier-mount-morgan.json');console.log(verifyDoc(d,attestation))" prints true. Every paid answer keeps its Telegraph signal hash, another team's arbitrator is shown as a cross-check (never as a record), and production conflicts are explained, not averaged.
