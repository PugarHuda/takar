Can I act on this answer?

An answer from one Telegraph miner is one opinion. Takar asks a second miner run by a different operator, compares the two field by field, writes who was right on chain, and lets money move only when the evidence agrees.

What you can use today

Free on the testnet: our escrow pays for the asks. Each call leaves a receipt you can check without trusting us.

A second opinion for any intent

Send an intent and its parameters. You get two answers from different operators, the fields where they differ, and both Telegraph signals. Disagreement is reported, never averaged.

curl -X POST https://takar-telegraph.vercel.app/api/second-opinion \
  -H 'content-type: application/json' \
  -d '{"intent":"SANCTIONS_SCREENING_MATCH_GENERAL",
       "params":{"name":"Bank Rossiya"},
       "fields":["matched"]}'

Escrow that waits for evidence

TakarSettle pays only when two operators agree and two resolvers sign. Either side can dispute with a bond; if both do, all three resolvers must sign.

What we don't claim

  • Everything runs on Ethereum Sepolia with test USDC. No real money has moved.
  • The fleet is simulated: real paid answers, made-up machines.
  • The scorecards cover a few dozen markets, and the conflict note covers one brief.
  • Our miner leaves vague questions unresolved rather than guess, so it answers fewer than a model would.
  • Independence is judged by fee address. Two addresses can still belong to one team; the registry cannot tell us.
  • Failures are in the repo next to the successes, including an evaluator the network rejected.
