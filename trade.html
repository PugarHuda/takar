Who gets paid when buyer and supplier disagree

A Trade Assurance-style order settled on Takar Settle. The buyer's payment sits in a contract. The supplier is paid only when independent specialists, bought through Telegraph, agree that the conditions are met. When they disagree, nothing moves until someone looks. Once either party disputes, the decision needs an outside seat held by another team (Praman) to back it.

The order is illustrative: the supplier is not a real company, and the amount is test USDC scaled 1:12,400. The ship, its AIS positions, the miners, every paid answer, the escrow and the bonds are real, on Ethereum Sepolia testnet.

The conditions, and who checks them

Delivered

Two vessel miners on different operators (Quay, a terrestrial AIS network; VesselWire, Fintraffic Digitraffic) both put the ship berthed at the destination port: within 1.5 km, under 1 knot, reading under 60 minutes old.

Invoice matches

Two reconcile miners on different operators both say the invoice reconciles with the purchase order and the goods-received note.

Supplier clear

Two sanctions-screening miners on different operators agree, and Praman's own OFAC SDN screen lands the same way.

Pay when all three pass. Hold while both vessel miners agree the ship has not arrived. Review when any pair disagrees or an answer is unusable: nothing is proposed. Refund when both reconcile miners say the invoice does not match, or the supplier is sanctioned. A proposal opens a 10-minute dispute window only after two of the three resolvers back the same decision on the same evidence. A party disputes by posting a 0.5 USDC bond, which goes to the other side if the decision stands.
